# KSRF Applicant Helper

Prepare a court complaint

A Russian-language workflow for people preparing a complaint to the Constitutional Court of the Russian Federation without a legal background. Start with your account of events, case documents, a case number, a draft complaint, or a letter from the Court Secretariat.

The assistant organizes documents and a timeline, explains possible constitutional issues, checks sources, and identifies missing evidence and objections. For revisions, it preserves the original and proposes a separate draft with an explanation of changes.

The package includes 16 skills covering initial assessment, legal research, drafting, review, document preparation, and possible next steps after a Court decision. OCR and working DOCX/PDF files are available only when the host provides the required execution and document tools. Available text can still be analyzed when those tools are missing.

Drafts require human review. Legal approval, signing, payment, and filing are not automated. The package does not promise that a complaint will be accepted or that a right will be restored.

ChatGPT, Codex, or another chosen host and connected services determine how messages and files are processed. Local file storage does not mean that the model runs entirely locally.

## Starter prompts

1. I am not a lawyer. Review my documents and explain how to start preparing a complaint to the Russian Constitutional Court.
2. Review my draft Constitutional Court complaint and propose a separate revision explaining the changes.
3. Explain this letter from the Court Secretariat: what must I correct and which documents do I need?
