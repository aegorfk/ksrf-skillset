## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Evidence-bound substantive review
The skill SHALL distinguish exact source evidence from agent inference, preserve author and adoption uncertainty, and connect premises to conclusions and consequences using full context.

#### Scenario: A party position is reproduced and rejected
- WHEN a source quotes a party and the court later rejects its decisive premise
- THEN the review retains the party attribution and rejection evidence rather than promoting the quotation to a court holding.

#### Scenario: A later source version changes the context
- WHEN the source binding differs from the packet used for analysis
- THEN the earlier review is treated as stale until the relevant evidence is revalidated.

### Requirement: Useful and discriminating method updates
The skill SHALL compare a proposed operation with the existing catalog and distinguish new operation, refinement, counterexample, illustration and no change.

#### Scenario: An additional act repeats a known operation
- WHEN the act adds no new condition, counterexample or correction of a demonstrated failure
- THEN the proposal remains an illustration or no change rather than a new general rule.

#### Scenario: No marker appears
- WHEN a full act lacks a lexical marker but contains a potentially useful reasoning operation
- THEN absence of a marker does not prevent substantive review or establish absence of argumentation.

### Requirement: Independent transfer evaluation
The skill SHALL assess Russian normative linkage separately from foreign similarity and keep evaluation inputs independent at matter-family level.

#### Scenario: Only an application error is established
- WHEN supplied material establishes breach of an existing safeguard but no normative defect
- THEN the review does not invent a constitutional issue and identifies the missing normative link or ordinary remedy inquiry.

#### Scenario: Lower-only or exposure evidence is missing
- WHEN a proposed held-out matter lacks verified lower-only sources or independence evidence
- THEN it remains unadmitted and the actual denominator is reported without substituting document or translation counts.
