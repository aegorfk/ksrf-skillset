## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Source-bound distinct argument operations
The comparative research skill SHALL expose necessity-before-balancing and norm-causation operations with verified source roles, locators and bounded domestic hypotheses.

#### Scenario: Technically decisive intrusive evidence
- **WHEN** an intrusive test could resolve a factual question but existing evidence and the governing presumption may already decide the legal issue
- **THEN** the skill separates legal need from later balancing, includes all affected persons, and does not equate a failure to rebut the presumption with permission to obtain new evidence
- **AND** it distinguishes authorization to obtain evidence from weight of an already lawfully obtained result.

#### Scenario: Interacting ceiling neutralizes challenged exclusion
- **WHEN** a claimant challenges exclusion of a payment but another applicable ceiling may produce the same result
- **THEN** the skill tests the causal contribution of the challenged provision and identifies missing calculation or legal linkage
- **AND** it does not treat a foreign inadmissibility decision as a merits finding or a directly applicable Russian procedural rule.

#### Scenario: Technical guidance and overbroad removal
- **WHEN** guidance recommends an individual assessment omitted by the governing legal rule
- **THEN** the skill investigates binding force, hierarchy and actual scope before finding a normative gap
- **AND** the proposed correction retains necessary safeguards instead of automatically removing every substantive threshold.

### Requirement: Honest source verification and domestic boundary
The skill SHALL retain acquisition provenance, exact quote bindings and the research-only status of unverified Russian bridges.

#### Scenario: Third-party report cross-check
- **WHEN** a retained third-party judgment is compared with an official court PDF
- **THEN** the provenance records both copies, the exact scope of the comparison and each hash
- **AND** a quote match does not assert byte identity or full equivalence of the two publications.
