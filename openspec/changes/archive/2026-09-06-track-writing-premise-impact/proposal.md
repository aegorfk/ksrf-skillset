## Why

The current writing runtime can reopen a finding only when its own sentence changes. It cannot record that an unchanged argument or requested remedy depends on a changed premise. This mechanical limitation is reproduced; an improvement in legal quality is still a hypothesis, not a measured result.

## What Changes

- Preserve optional, reasoned sentence-dependency declarations and their explicit retirement history.
- After revisions, identify direct and transitive review targets, including unchanged sentences without argument cards or previous findings.
- Reopen affected findings and create an impact-review item without editing conclusions, deleting alternatives or granting legal approval.
- Add a focused method for distinguishing a changed own premise from an objection to an opponent, conditional reserve reasoning and independent grounds.
- Keep real-source review private; publish no source texts or claimed legal-effectiveness score.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

None.

### Modified Capabilities

- `ksrf-writing-loop`: declared cross-sentence dependencies and review propagation after a revision.

## Impact

The writing runtime, its existing reference, complaint QA routing, source-only tests and release manifest. Old payloads remain usable. No new library, service, filing gate or automatic legal decision is introduced. Canonical installation occurs only after verified publication.
