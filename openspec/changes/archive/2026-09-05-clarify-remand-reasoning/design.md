# Design

Keep the existing reference as the single owner. A remand analysis records the
express legal conclusion, the identified defect, issues requiring further
consideration, and the later court's treatment. It checks an inference from an
unchosen disposition against the court's actual reasoning and the applicable
procedural powers, instead of treating that counterfactual as judicial speech.

The output helps the drafter identify which court actually supplied the challenged
criterion, preserves the strongest competing reading, and narrows the requested
correction without deciding missing facts. Source review must not silently turn
an adverse example into authority for the applicant's preferred interpretation.

Source discovery starts with the real complaint and proceeds to full acts. A
missing act limits only the dependent conclusion. A cited remand can be analyzed
without pretending the full lower-court packet is available. The original
complaint's outcome is already known; the review is retrospective and is neither
a Langfuse/DeepEval comparison nor a native promotion receipt.

All existing public/private, source, human veto, installation and publication
boundaries remain unchanged. Checks distinguish technical integrity from source
review and from unperformed human legal approval.
