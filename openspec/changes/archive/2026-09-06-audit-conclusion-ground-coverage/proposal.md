## Why

The writing runtime tracks only declared influence paths. Its impact context is not a complete inventory of surviving grounds, and absence of an edge is not reviewed independence. The current instructions name these limits but do not give the drafter a focused backward-review procedure.

## What Changes

- Test H1: starting from each material conclusion and requested result exposes missing intermediate propositions and limits the review to a stated scope.
- Test H2: reconstructing surviving support, separately for cumulative, alternative and conditional reasoning, prevents unsupported preservation and premature deletion of a reserve.
- Add a focused methodological reference and entry points from writing and QA. Reuse existing source, causal and remedy methods instead of introducing another legal gate or graph engine.
- Record source-audit limits and release evidence without publishing source documents or reconstructive derivatives.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

None.

### Modified Capabilities

- `ksrf-writing-loop`: explicit backward coverage review and reasoned survival review, independent from automatic impact propagation.

## Impact

Skill instructions and references, README, OpenSpec and release manifest only. No CLI/schema changes, new services, LLM benchmark, automatic legal classification or changes to filing authority. Existing runtime and its regression tests remain unchanged.
