## Legal method

Build a stage-by-stage account of what question the court decided, what normative
operation it used, which facts it treated as established, and what consequence
followed. Keep a party's contention, a quoted precedent and the court's own reason
separate. Later affirmance is not by itself adoption of every earlier reason.

Test whether an alternative ground is legally and factually sufficient on its own
within the actual record, or depends on the challenged norm, evidentiary burden,
or an already superseded finding. Do not remove an express use from the factual
account merely because outcome causation is weak. Do not invent a hypothetical
ground the courts did not establish to reject the applicant's line.

Use a counterfactual to assess the scope of the causal claim, not to assert that
the applicant must win after the norm is corrected. Procedural harm, merits harm,
and a possible opportunity for reconsideration must be analyzed at their own level.

The practical output is a short causal paragraph and a strongest objection with
exact locators and a concrete evidence task where needed, not an additional schema.

## Verification and release

Use independent legal review and a separate input-only agent on newly prepared
synthetic records. Do not claim general legal accuracy from a small qualitative
check. Preserve actual outputs and distinguish observed behavior from a rubric.
Source-only fixtures and evaluator material stay out of the installed skills.

runtime installation. Archive the validated OpenSpec change. Publish one atomic
manifest-bound commit to canonical main, check live SHA, and only then install
the verified bytes globally. The recurrence continues with another useful topic;
no cosmetic commit is required when the current method is already sufficient.
