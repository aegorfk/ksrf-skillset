## Why

The skills track norm use, causation and preservation separately, but complaint
writers still need a concrete way to turn the complete chain of judicial reasons
into a defensible paragraph. A cited rule, a surviving ground, an independent
alternative and an unresolved incorporation must not become interchangeable.

## What Changes

- Add a concise substantive method for comparing reasons across judicial stages,
  checking genuinely independent grounds and drafting a bounded causal bridge.
- Ground the method in full official decisions, including limiting examples, and
  current statutory requirements rather than treating technical labels as law.
- Audit overlap and any ambiguous existing instruction about non-application;
  clarify only proven gaps without introducing new runtime gates or schemas.
- Add conditional integration, synthetic input-only verification, and a short
  user-facing explanation of the practical drafting improvement.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- `ksrf-cross-instance-causal-drafting`: Source-bound complaint drafting from
  changing, concurrent and independent reasons across judicial instances.

### Modified Capabilities

None planned. If legal accuracy requires a runtime contract change, identify it
explicitly before implementation rather than silently contradicting that contract.

## Impact

One targeted legal reference, narrow links and clarifications in existing skills,
source-only verification material, README and the standard release manifest.
No private complaint source, automated filing or new runtime command.
