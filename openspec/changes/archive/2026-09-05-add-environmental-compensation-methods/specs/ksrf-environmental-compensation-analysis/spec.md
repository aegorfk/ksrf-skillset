## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Separate restoration, compensation and constitutional defect
The skillset SHALL provide a reachable two-pass method that distinguishes stopping harm, restoration, residual harm and monetary compensation, verifies normative causality and preserves public environmental interests.

#### Scenario: Restoration is documented but its effect is unknown
- **WHEN** an applicant supplies expenditure records without proof of the achieved environmental condition
- **THEN** the method requires evidence of the result and reasonable necessary costs, without declaring automatic credit or stopping unrelated research

#### Scenario: The alleged defect is only an accounting dispute
- **WHEN** a current rule permits individual assessment and the dispute concerns only rejected invoices
- **THEN** the method distinguishes ordinary evidentiary review from a demonstrated constitutional defect

### Requirement: Preserve historical decision boundaries
The skillset SHALL distinguish party arguments from holdings, the challenged provisions from background law, and constitutional success from individual restoration.

#### Scenario: A party asks to invalidate every challenged provision
- **WHEN** the historical result preserves the delegation to use standard assessment formulas and excludes the unchallenged budget provisions
- **THEN** the method does not report total success or an obligation to credit every expense

#### Scenario: Reopening is conditional
- **WHEN** the historical operative part ties reconsideration to a verified restoration condition
- **THEN** the method requests evidence of that condition and current procedural grounds instead of promising automatic reopening or a refund

### Requirement: Publish attribution without complaint originals
Public documentation SHALL credit source-confirmed professional roles with active links and distinguish a case report from a full complaint. Synthetic checks SHALL be self-contained and original-free.

#### Scenario: Only a professional case report is publicly located
- **WHEN** the report names the representatives and preparation team but does not expose the complaint
- **THEN** documentation labels that report accurately, leaves the full-text URL gap explicit and does not invent authors or links

#### Scenario: Clean-room user has no private corpus
- **WHEN** the skillset is installed in a separate empty directory
- **THEN** the method and its routes remain available while complaint originals, extraction files and maintainer evals are absent
