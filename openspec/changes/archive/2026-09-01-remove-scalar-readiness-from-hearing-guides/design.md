# Design: Preserve hearing heuristics without a compensating legal score

## Context

The three guides are useful qualitative libraries. Their current score vocabulary creates a second, inconsistent readiness system alongside `evaluation-and-promotion.md` and `ksrf-complaint-qa`: positive indicators can be read as compensating for absent application, evidence, exhaustion, or remedy. The change must retain the diagnostic content while removing that inference.

## Decisions

1. Use one shared, Russian-language status vocabulary:
   - `подтверждено` — the named positive criterion is supported by identified material;
   - `предупреждение` — the named anti-pattern or adverse fact is present;
   - `недостаточно данных` — the criterion cannot be resolved and remains a collection or verification task.
2. Never total, average, weight, or convert these statuses into readiness. One confirmed dimension cannot cure another warning, missing record, or failed hard gate.
3. Rename exactly 15, 14, and 11 per-pattern labels in the three guides to `Проверка по признакам`, while rewriting only score-bearing words around the preserved positive and adverse criteria.
4. Preserve the six dimensions in the first final rubric and five dimensions in the second, but express each as independent confirmed/warning/insufficient-data conditions.
5. Preserve the eleven technique sections and their three operational bullets. Rewrite all four score/risk occurrences, expressed through three lexical forms (`Снизить оценку`, `Снизить риск`, `Снижать оценку`), as explicit confirmation or warning instructions.
6. Keep `evaluation-and-promotion.md` and `ksrf-complaint-qa` authoritative for comparison and gates; the hearing guides remain research-derived stress-test aids.

## Preservation contract

- All numbered argument-pattern and constitutional-justification headings remain in order.
- All eleven technique codes, corpus hit counts, marker fragments, drafting formulas, signals, links, and complaint packages remain.
- Both consuming skills retain their exact backlinks.
- All three guides remain in the canonical payload.
- Ordinary legal uses of `автоматизм` and `автоматический` remain; the cleanup does not use a broad `автомат*` ban.

## Verification

- RED proves the current labels, score verbs, 0–2 scales, and summed readiness ranges still exist.
- GREEN proves the exact check counts, independent status contract, preserved dimension names, technique codes, sections, backlinks, and payload membership.
- Full relevant tests, source strict, clean-room runtime strict, strict OpenSpec validation, exact manifest verification, diff check, and independent review gate publication.

## Risks and controls

- **A useful adverse criterion disappears.** Controlled by exact positive/adverse phrase assertions and structural preservation checks.
- **The new statuses become another score.** Controlled by explicit non-compensation text and forbidden total/average/weight language.
- **A real automatic extraction capability is accidentally removed.** Controlled by limiting the change to qualitative Markdown and preserving all operational bullets; no script or schema changes.
- **Generic automatic-language cleanup damages legal doctrine.** Controlled by exact forbidden markers and no-overmatch assertions for legitimate `автоматизм` text.
