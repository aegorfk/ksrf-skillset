# Design

## Method and source boundaries

Extend the existing decisive-premise and independent-guarantee methods rather than replace the complaint workflow. The first pass uses current-case evidence only. The second pass compares a verified public position and the relevant reasoning and operative clauses of Resolution No. 3-P of 28 January 2025.

Qualification, lost safeguards and individual effects are separate links. Similar effects do not prove identical legal regimes. Registry information, physical facts and legal presumptions are not interchangeable.

Knowledge requires a specific actor, content, date and rule of attribution. Applicability of a time limit, its starting event and the legal effect of expiration require separate treatment. Each case branch keeps its own application, exhaustion and remedy conditions.

Before claiming novelty, compare norm/version, category, issue and operative scope of a prior holding. Distinguish implementation of existing protection, a supported unresolved aspect and insufficient information. A wrong source identity or unsupported proposition must not be silently repaired.

## Provenance

The confirmed private file is usable for method extraction, not proof of historical filing, sole authorship or success. Public credits use the official decision and the Centre's first-party account of Ozova's representation and the team's preparation of the oral position. Do not present that webpage as a full complaint or treat the related decision as the proven outcome of the private file.

## Evaluation

Four argument-builder scenarios and two QA scenarios use invented non-land facts and contain all needed evidence. A contrastive pair tests both absent and expressly established legal attribution of knowledge. They are source-only development inputs, not a model-performance result. Regressions check routes, privacy, source-role separation and installation. Preserve unrelated baseline failures and report them separately.

## Release

Use a clean worktree at live main, verify global payload parity, synchronize all canonical skills and regenerate the manifest. Publish the explicit atomic scope, verify live remote SHA, then install and verify the published global payload. The original private source is archived unchanged only after methodology extraction.
