## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Qualification-dependent safeguards
The workflow MUST trace the factual premise, applied qualification, excluded safeguard and individual consequence separately. It MUST NOT infer identical legal regimes solely from similar consequences or treat a proxy as the required fact.

#### Scenario: The label changes but the burden remains
- **WHEN** two legal routes cause similar harm but have different preconditions
- **THEN** the analysis tests the specific safeguard independently and preserves material differences between the routes

#### Scenario: A formal proxy does not prove the legal premise
- **WHEN** a draft infers a factual condition from the absence of a record or physical marker
- **THEN** the analysis identifies the missing factual bridge and checks whether the applied rule actually permits that inference

### Requirement: Attributable knowledge and separate branches
The workflow MUST distinguish the applicability of a temporal guarantee from its starting event and effect. It MUST identify the actor, content, date and legal basis for attributing knowledge and MUST preserve separate case-specific application, time and remedy conditions.

#### Scenario: Different bodies received different information
- **WHEN** one public body received a notice before the competent body
- **THEN** the analysis does not automatically select the earlier date without the applicable attribution rule and evidence

#### Scenario: An express attribution rule supplies the starting event
- **WHEN** a verified rule expressly attributes one body's receipt of specific information to the relevant authority
- **THEN** the analysis applies that rule rather than demanding separate firsthand awareness, while preserving unknown conditions for calculating expiration

### Requirement: Existing holdings and citation repair
The workflow MUST compare a relevant prior holding by norm version, issue, category and operative scope before claiming novelty. It MUST distinguish enforcement of existing protection, an evidenced unresolved issue and an unknown comparison. A faulty citation MUST NOT be silently replaced or used for a dependent legal claim.

#### Scenario: A prior judgment covers only one aspect
- **WHEN** the draft claims the entire issue is new but a verified judgment supplies protection for one category
- **THEN** the analysis rejects that novelty claim, preserves investigation of a materially different aspect and does not promise admissibility

#### Scenario: Correct-looking metadata points to another subject
- **WHEN** a cited decision has a different date or subject and does not support the stated proposition
- **THEN** the analysis records the mismatch, withholds the dependent attribution and identifies what must be verified without inventing a replacement authority

### Requirement: Portable and private-source-safe learning
The release MUST keep private documents and reconstructive derivatives outside the public payload. Its first-pass method and synthetic scenarios MUST work without those documents, and historical outcomes MUST be confined to a separate comparison pass.

#### Scenario: Independent user lacks the original
- **WHEN** an independent user opens the reference or development scenarios
- **THEN** the material remains self-contained and does not assert the authorship, court adoption or success of a private draft

### Requirement: Verified participant credits
Public source credits MUST distinguish representation, preparation of an oral position, authorship of a specific complaint version and judicial adoption. A related public case MUST NOT establish the filing or outcome of an unidentified private version.

#### Scenario: The public source confirms representation and team work
- **WHEN** a first-party account confirms a team's preparation of the oral position and the decision identifies the representative
- **THEN** the release credits those specific roles with active source and decision links without inferring sole authorship or publication of the private complaint
