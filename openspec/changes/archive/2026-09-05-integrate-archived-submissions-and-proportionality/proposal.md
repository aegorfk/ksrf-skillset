## Why

New institutional opinions and written submissions recovered from the Wayback Machine make competing arguments in the same proceeding available for comparison. A newly supplied dissertation supports a focused review of proportionality, but neither archival recovery nor doctrinal authority proves a judicial holding or model effectiveness.

## What Changes

- Review seven submissions, distinguish two publisher versions, and perform a locator-bound methodological reading of the dissertation.
- Extend canonical KSRF references only where the review adds a substantive distinction to existing methods.
- Credit original publishers, archived snapshots, verified document roles and the separate final judicial act.
- Add self-contained synthetic scenarios and source-only regression checks without publishing source documents.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities
- `ksrf-archived-submission-methods`: provenance-aware comparison of opposing submissions and bounded doctrinal transfer.

## Impact

Global KSRF methodological references, related entrypoints, public source documentation, synthetic evaluation assets and their tests. No parser, legal approval, filing automation or live LLM run. Original PDFs, OCR and local review records remain private.
