## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Repeated submissions retain distinct source and outcome identities
The method MUST verify the document date, applicant, procedural history, challenged provisions and prayer before linking a complaint to a decision. A prior act cited inside a later complaint MUST NOT be labelled as that document's subsequent outcome.

#### Scenario: A later complaint quotes the suggested result
- **WHEN** the supplied complaint discusses an earlier refusal and contains later procedural events and additional challenged provisions
- **THEN** the method distinguishes the prior act from the later outcome and preserves any uncertainty about the exact filed version

### Requirement: A renewed constitutional argument requires more than a renamed aspect
The method MUST separately test the actual difference from the prior issue, the availability and effect of alternative procedures, application of each challenged provision, and the personal factual basis of every remedy branch. It MUST preserve existing admissibility and human-decision requirements.

#### Scenario: Additional norms describe unused alternatives
- **WHEN** the draft adds appellate, cassation or reopening provisions without records demonstrating application
- **THEN** the method distinguishes contextual alternatives from challenged provisions and does not treat a longer norm list as a cured refusal

#### Scenario: An urgent protection claim does not establish its factual premise
- **WHEN** a prayer invokes an absence of a judicial decision but supplied records establish only disagreement with an existing decision
- **THEN** the method requests evidence of the claimed condition rather than converting the hypothetical branch into a personal fact

### Requirement: Publication preserves attribution and source-free validation
The release MUST credit the verified complaint representative and professional donor with active complaint-source, professional and distinct prior/final-act links. The public method and synthetic evals MUST exclude original files, full extracted text and reconstructive derivatives.

#### Scenario: The recipient has no historical source files
- **WHEN** the runtime is installed and the source-only synthetic cases are reviewed
- **THEN** the method remains self-contained, eval assets stay outside runtime and validation is not described as proven judicial or model effectiveness
