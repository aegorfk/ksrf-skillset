## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Public-owner disputes use a role and reliance matrix

The skillset SHALL require a `PublicOwnerRelianceAndRiskMatrix` that separately records the disputed asset and title history, the public claimant and source of its title, the public body's inventory and registration duties, state-confirmed transactions, information available to the acquirer at the transaction date, concrete red flags, due-diligence steps, burden of proof, duration and cause of public inaction, individualized public and private interests, causal contribution, less restrictive remedies, and the requested constitutional boundary before treating a public-owner property conflict as drafting-ready.

#### Scenario: Public owner invokes ordinary vindication rules

- **WHEN** a public-law entity seeks property from a later acquirer under the same abstract rule available to a private owner
- **THEN** the skillset separately tests the entity's title, public control and registration powers, timely performance, causal contribution and the concrete public interest instead of assuming identical positions

#### Scenario: Acquirer relied on official registration

- **WHEN** an acquirer invokes an official registry entry or state-confirmed chain of transactions
- **THEN** the skillset records the exact entry, transaction date, accessible warnings and verification steps, and treats reliance as a significant but not conclusive factor

### Requirement: Knowledge and risk remain time-bound and evidence-bound

The skillset SHALL distinguish information available to the acquirer at the transaction date from fraud, criminal findings, defects or public claims discovered later. Later knowledge SHALL NOT be back-projected without evidence, while an official registry entry SHALL NOT erase concrete red flags that were objectively accessible at the relevant time.

#### Scenario: Fraud is established after acquisition

- **WHEN** a later criminal judgment establishes forged documents or an unlawful first transfer
- **THEN** the skillset does not infer that the final acquirer knew or should have known at purchase without a separate time-specific evidence chain

### Requirement: Public inaction and alternatives are decomposed

The skillset SHALL separately test identification and inventory duties, title registration, subsequent state confirmations, duration of inaction, the public body's ability to prevent or detect the defect, claims against wrongdoers, compensation or substitute-asset options, and the causal relationship between public conduct and the applicant's loss.

#### Scenario: Public body delayed formalizing title

- **WHEN** a public claimant could identify and register inherited or otherwise public property but acted only after several transactions
- **THEN** the skillset requires dates, competent bodies, applicable duties, omissions, causation and alternative protective measures before assigning the loss to the acquirer

### Requirement: Partial constitutional outcomes remain separate

The skillset SHALL preserve accepted, rejected, narrowed and unaddressed complaint routes separately. A successful constitutional boundary on public-owner risk allocation SHALL NOT be presented as validation of a distinct legal-certainty challenge or as an automatic finding that the applicant was factually in good faith.

#### Scenario: One of two complaint theories succeeds

- **WHEN** the later official act accepts the public-owner risk-allocation theory but upholds the evaluative definition of good-faith acquisition
- **THEN** the retrospective card and QA report identify the two outcomes separately and do not describe the complaint as wholly accepted

### Requirement: Retrospective calibration remains source-separated

The installed skillset SHALL include a public-safe two-pass retrospective card for Constitutional Court Judgment No. 16-P/2017 and SHALL exclude the private complaint, written explanations, full-text derivatives, scans, page images and local paths. The card SHALL identify public sources and people by proven role and SHALL NOT infer individual complaint authorship from a signature or document metadata alone.

#### Scenario: User has no historical private source

- **WHEN** a user installs the public skillset without the complaint or written explanations
- **THEN** the matrix, checklist and synthetic scenarios remain usable for a new matter while private-source hashes serve only as provenance for the published method card
