## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Functional status and cascade analysis
The methodology MUST distinguish the exact statutory condition, the legal function of a supporting document, the wording used by a person, subordinate guidance and each downstream consequence. It MUST NOT treat a shared label or the invalidation of multiple documents as self-sufficient proof of a constitutional defect.

#### Scenario: A document proves a related legal fact
- **WHEN** a person uses a common status label and an official certificate proves a related legal fact
- **THEN** the workflow traces the relevant statutory system and function of the certificate before proposing any constitutional conclusion

#### Scenario: One alleged defect affects every signature sheet
- **WHEN** the same description produces invalidation of every supporting signature
- **THEN** the workflow maps the exact rule, administrative or judicial transformation, available correction mechanism, individual effect and less restrictive alternatives without presuming invalidity of the result

### Requirement: Normative-defect and ordinary-error boundary
The methodology MUST separately assess a one-off judicial error, a norm-enabled judicial meaning, established practice and an exceptional practice-forming risk. Institutional reports and speeches MAY guide research questions but MUST NOT become binding authority.

#### Scenario: An official report describes constitutional control
- **WHEN** a report or speech describes the Court's methods or institutional role
- **THEN** the workflow records it as a methodological signal and still requires current primary legal sources for any admissibility, holding or remedy claim

### Requirement: Time-sensitive post-decision remedy
The methodology MUST distinguish concluded time-sensitive relations from the legal basis and form of an individual remedy. It MUST verify the exact dispositive text, current law, competent forum, injury, causation and requested relief before suggesting compensation.

#### Scenario: Review cannot restore an expired opportunity
- **WHEN** the Court's act indicates that review cannot restore a time-limited right and refers to compensatory mechanisms
- **THEN** the workflow does not promise a particular award or procedure and prepares a separate evidence-bound route for the applicable mechanism

### Requirement: Private originals and portable validation
The release MUST exclude original complaints, scans, OCR and reconstructive derivatives while retaining confirmed public source links and fully self-contained synthetic scenarios.

#### Scenario: A user has no source complaint
- **WHEN** a user runs the supplied scenarios without access to a private archive
- **THEN** every required fact is fictional and contained in the scenario, and the result does not claim independent outcome validation
