## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Scope a legal counterexample to the actual enforcement objection

The enforcement methodology SHALL distinguish the refutation of an alleged necessary condition from the independent justification of the requested protection, using source-bound adversarial materials without importing another procedure's conditions.

#### Scenario: The objection asserts that termination requires cancellation
- **WHEN** a supplied objection treats cancellation of the judgment as necessary for termination of enforcement
- **THEN** the method checks the scope and context of that assertion and any verified counterexample
- **AND** a counterexample removes only the asserted universal dependency, not the need to justify protection in the applicant's case

#### Scenario: The objection denies an automatic effect
- **WHEN** a source denies automatic termination or a specific statutory ground
- **THEN** the method does not rewrite that narrower statement as universal impossibility or an admission of the applicant's entire position

#### Scenario: A settlement is used as a counterexample
- **WHEN** the historical applicants' response invokes a settlement during enforcement
- **THEN** the method preserves the distinct legal basis and consent conditions and does not import them into a dispute lacking those conditions
- **AND** current authority, constitutional necessity, competent procedure and remaining creditor interests still require separate verification

#### Scenario: Source and test limits remain visible
- **WHEN** the clarification is distributed
- **THEN** it links the real objection and response with their separate roles and page locators without distributing source documents
- **AND** editorial tests are not represented as a legal-quality benchmark or permission to file
