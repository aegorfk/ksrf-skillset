## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Conditional purpose reasoning preserves the open premise

The argument reference SHALL distinguish a supported lawful purpose from a purpose
assumed solely for a conditional reserve argument against the chosen means.

#### Scenario: Purpose is disputed
- **WHEN** a source-bound purpose is assumed for a reserve analysis
- **THEN** the text SHALL preserve its conditional status and the main objection
- **AND** SHALL NOT certify the purpose, its legitimacy or individual misconduct.

#### Scenario: Means require separate proof
- **WHEN** the reserve argument challenges suitability, necessity or burden
- **THEN** each selected defect SHALL have its own grounds
- **AND** any alternative SHALL be compared against the same conditional purpose
- **AND** the defect of the means SHALL NOT prove that the purpose is illegitimate.

#### Scenario: Full balancing remains incomplete
- **WHEN** the purpose is not established outside the conditional argument
- **THEN** the purpose card and existing QA SHALL retain the uncertainty
- **AND** conditional reasoning SHALL NOT pass a full balancing or filing gate.

#### Scenario: A real submission supplies a method signal
- **WHEN** the method is reviewed against a published complaint
- **THEN** the review SHALL separate applicant reasoning from court authority
- **AND** SHALL NOT publish the original, reconstructive derivatives or invented facts
- **AND** SHALL NOT claim an outcome-blind or comparative generation evaluation.
