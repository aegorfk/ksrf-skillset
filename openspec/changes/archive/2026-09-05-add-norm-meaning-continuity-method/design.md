## Approach

The new reference lives with the complaint cycle's official-source and norm-version
guidance. It is a substantive reading and drafting method, not a machine decision
procedure. Apply it when a relevant rule was amended, repealed, replaced or renumbered,
or acquired a different established judicial meaning;
do not require a separate record in unrelated cases.

## Legal reasoning

1. Establish the material dates, every relied-on judicial act, controlling edition,
   amendment's effective date, transition rule and present procedural stage.
2. Compare addressees, triggers, exceptions, legal effects, temporal provisions and
   judicial meaning. Keep unknowns explicit and permit material changes outside the
   challenged mechanism; do not equate identical text with identical legal effect.
3. Independently assess past application, continuing application to earlier legal
   relations, persistent harm and whether the successor addresses the actual defect.
   A general temporal rule and established dates may suffice; do not universally
   demand a special transitional clause, new judicial act or pending procedure.
4. Draw a bounded conclusion on identified elements and a separate admissibility
   assessment using current official law. A method result neither resets time limits
   nor proves exhaustion, application, merits or an available remedy.
5. Draft the link from the exact applied rule to individual harm and the requested
   consequence. Produce a strongest objection and identify the evidence needed to
   resolve it. Never silently substitute the successor rule for the applied edition.

## Source and historical boundaries

The 2017 doctrinal card explains the origin of the research question, not current
Russian procedure. Verify the current statutory provisions and read full official
decisions before using their outcomes. In particular, distinguish the rule on
repealed acts from any rule on termination of proceedings already instituted; do not
reuse a historical passage without confirming its current status and procedural role.

Cases must include a limiting or adverse result. A judgment reaching the merits of
an old rule does not prove that repeal establishes unconstitutionality, and a remedy
ordered in one case does not become a generic remedy. Store public-document copies
outside Git; publish only concise methodological summaries, precise official links,
locators and hashes. No private complaints or reconstructive derivatives are used.

## Integration

Use short conditional links in the complaint-cycle, triage, QA and authority-builder
entrypoints. Correct the two existing continuing-effect descriptions without changing
runtime enums or gates. Historical provenance remains intact; only the separately
validated continuity method gets an active route. Other doctrine candidates retain
their status. Do not turn the 103-card corpus into an implicitly promoted corpus.

## Verification

Evaluate legal reasoning, not strings or headings. Synthetic cases should vary
continuing application and remaining harm independently, test genuine correction,
renumbering, partial change, temporal uncertainty and procedural barriers. An
independent agent receives only a realistic request, the candidate reference and raw
case materials, without the expected answer or implementation rationale. Record its
actual answer and assess against an independently held rubric. This is a bounded
behavioral check, not a success-rate estimate or professional certification.

No standalone model/provider benchmark or production-prompt selection is in scope.
Run strict skill/source/privacy and clean-install checks, archive the validated
OpenSpec change, then publish an atomic release and install only verified live bytes.
