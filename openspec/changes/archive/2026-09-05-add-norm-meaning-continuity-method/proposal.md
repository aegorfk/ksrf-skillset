## Why

Users need a legal method for deciding what a statutory amendment actually changes
for a constitutional complaint. Existing guidance can conflate an individual harm
that remains with continued application of the former rule; the historical Bondar
continuity card has not yet become a case-tested working method.

## What Changes

- Add one Russian, source-bound methodological reference comparing six elements of
  the former and successor rules, not merely their wording or numbering.
- Separate past judicial application, continued application to earlier relations,
  remaining individual harm, and the new rule's possible correction of the mechanism.
- Connect the method conditionally to version review, triage, authority research and
  complaint QA; keep current admissibility and source requirements independent.
- Include official positive and limiting cases, synthetic drafting examples and
  counterexamples, and independently evaluated unseen inputs.
- Preserve the historical status of the other two doctrine deltas and the existing
  103-card corpus. Clarify only the dated integration of the continuity method.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- `ksrf-norm-meaning-continuity`: Legal analysis of changed, repealed, replaced or
  renumbered rules for a constitutional complaint, with explicit temporal limits.

### Modified Capabilities

None. Runtime admissibility derivation, schemas, CLI and release controls are unchanged.

## Impact

One installed reference, scoped links and wording in existing skills, synthetic
source-only evaluation materials, README and the normal release manifest. No new
runtime code, dependencies, command, approval system, private corpus or filing action.
