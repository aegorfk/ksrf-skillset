## Why

Three examples installed with `ksrf-explore-arguments` explain themselves as benchmarks, forward tests, eval contours, replays, fixtures, and committed artifacts. The runtime does not ship a benchmark harness, and that maintainer vocabulary obscures the useful method: separate what follows from the initial material, freeze hypotheses before reading the later KSRF act, then compare without hindsight. A user should receive that method and a checklist, not instructions for an unavailable evaluation laboratory.

## What Changes

- Replace maintainer/evaluation vocabulary in the three retrospective examples with plain Russian two-pass analysis language.
- Retain the anti-hindsight sequence: initial material first, frozen hypotheses and gaps second, later official act third.
- Rename regression rubrics as non-scoring checklists whose questions are checked independently as `подтверждено`, `пробел`, or `не применимо`.
- Keep the warning that the cards were prepared after the later acts and therefore neither predict a new case nor serve as complaint templates.
- Preserve case-specific facts, norms, hypotheses, remedies, adverse/falsifier material, source warnings, official URLs, provenance hashes, and private-source boundaries.
- Separate example-specific control points from transferable questions and require a new matter to be checked against its own primary materials.
- Require every substantive later-act statement to carry an official locator, actor, context, and norm-version boundary or remain explicitly unverified and unusable as authority.
- Update the owning skill's routing paragraph and the exact public documentation files `README.md`, `docs/KSRF_SKILLS_METHODOLOGY.md`, and `docs/KSRF_PROJECT_WORK_AND_PUBLIC_SOURCES.md`.
- Keep `evals/evals.json` and `evals/trigger-evals.json` source-only, unchanged, validated separately, and absent from user installation.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

None.

### Modified Capabilities

- `ksrf-runtime-methodology-truthfulness`: installed examples must teach a user-executable method and must not present an unshipped maintainer benchmark workflow as runtime functionality.
