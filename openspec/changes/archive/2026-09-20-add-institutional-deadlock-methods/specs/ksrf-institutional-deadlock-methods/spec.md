## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Joint-effect and safeguard analysis
The workflow MUST distinguish the validity of a decision rule from the absence of a mechanism to resolve prolonged inability to reach a decision. It MUST trace the applied norm, related rules, duration, individual harm, counterarguments and available exit mechanisms before inferring a constitutional defect.

#### Scenario: A continuity guarantee perpetuates a deadlock
- **WHEN** a voting rule and a holdover provision jointly prevent a newly elected body from exercising its mandate
- **THEN** the workflow tests both the justification for the threshold and the independent need for an exit mechanism without presuming that simple majority is constitutionally mandatory

#### Scenario: The law already provides an effective exit
- **WHEN** a timely and applicable fallback exists but has not been used
- **THEN** the workflow tests that fallback and does not assert a regulatory omission from political disagreement alone

### Requirement: Distinct roles for interconnected norms
The workflow MUST keep direct application, systemic context and reproduced superior content separate. It MUST NOT infer admissibility for every interconnected provision or declare a contextual provision invalid merely because it contributes to the combined effect.

#### Scenario: One provision is contextual and another reproduces a superior rule
- **WHEN** the record proves direct application only of the enabling norm
- **THEN** the workflow identifies the evidentiary gap for separate challenges and examines the source of reproduced content rather than expanding the subject automatically

### Requirement: Aspect-specific outcome and deferred review
The workflow MUST distinguish an upheld aspect, a missing safeguard, terminated review and conditional individual consequences within one judgment.

#### Scenario: Review follows legislative correction
- **WHEN** the operative clause conditions individual review on legislative change
- **THEN** the execution plan checks whether that condition has been fulfilled and identifies the current procedure without promising immediate cancellation or treating a historical delay as current fact

### Requirement: Attribution and portable private-source learning
The release MUST identify the confirmed author using active verified professional and outcome links while keeping private correspondence, complaints and reconstructive derivatives outside the public payload. Synthetic evaluation inputs MUST be self-contained and omit historical identifiers.

#### Scenario: A user has no source complaint
- **WHEN** the public method and synthetic tasks are installed or inspected independently
- **THEN** they remain usable without the private source, and structural validation is reported separately from model performance
