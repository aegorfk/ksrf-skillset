## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Omit public missing-link notices without falsifying provenance
Public descriptions of studied complaints SHALL present confirmed information and links without notices or placeholders announcing missing public complaint-source or donor-channel links. Internal attribution records SHALL preserve unknown and unavailable statuses and the existing basis for public credit.

#### Scenario: Private complaint has no confirmed public URL
- **WHEN** an authorized public attribution describes a privately supplied complaint
- **THEN** the description omits the unavailable link and the gap notice while retaining confirmed credit and existing accurate links
- **AND** the internal complaint-source status remains unchanged

#### Scenario: Professional profile exists without a complaint text
- **WHEN** a professional profile is the available public link
- **THEN** it remains labeled as a profile or professional channel and is not presented as the complaint text

#### Scenario: Prior instructions required a public notice
- **WHEN** publication instructions or reference examples contain that requirement
- **THEN** they are aligned with the omission rule without removing private-source safeguards, legal-source verification or final-act status checks
