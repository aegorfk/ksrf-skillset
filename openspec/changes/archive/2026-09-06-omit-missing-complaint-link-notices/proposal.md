## Why
Public material lists should credit verified sources without repetitive notices about missing public complaint URLs. The user's editorial instruction supersedes the prior requirement to print that gap.

## What Changes
- Omit missing-link notices from public source descriptions and method examples.
- Preserve internal source-status tracking, authorization for public credit and truthful link labels.
- Align publication instructions and regression tests; do not publish originals or invent links.

## Capabilities
### New Capabilities
- `ksrf-public-source-presentation`: concise public attribution without missing-link placeholders.
### Modified Capabilities

## Impact
Publication documentation, source-attribution guidance, affected method references and source-only tests. No parser, filing, source-access or adjudication behavior changes.
