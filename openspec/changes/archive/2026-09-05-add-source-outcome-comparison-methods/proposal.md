# Source-to-outcome comparison

## Why

The selected Sutyajnik complaints and SPbGU opinions contain materially different document roles and outcomes. They improve complaint preparation only when an argument is compared with its own judicial response, not treated as successful because an expert supported it.

## What Changes

- Add a reusable two-pass method for procedural obstacles, benefit comparators, and conditional remedies.
- Add a distinct expert-opinion comparison method covering regulatory uncertainty, transitional periods and competing interpretations.
- Extend self-contained synthetic evaluation scenarios and source-only regression tests.
- Credit public sources with complaint/opinion, professional publisher and decision links, without republishing source documents.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities
- `ksrf-source-outcome-comparison`: role-aware comparison and bounded methodological transfer.

## Impact

Canonical KSRF skills, their public source documentation and maintainer evaluation assets. No parser, filing service or approval behaviour changes. Originals and textual derivatives remain private. No claim of measured model improvement follows from structural tests.
