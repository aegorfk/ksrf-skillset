# Design

Use two references owned by doctrine research and rights argument building. First pass derives a hypothesis from the new case without a known outcome; second pass checks historical comparators, source roles and limits. Preserve every existing route and evaluation entry.

Complaints, supplements, an organisation's intake review, institutional opinions and a Supreme Court response are separate roles. A deidentified source is not deanonymised; a matching issue is not proof of its filing or of the Court's reliance on the opinion. Case-level correspondence may be established from independently published procedural details, without restoring redacted names. Unresolved templates remain unresolved and do not block independent analysis.

Public artifacts contain original methodological synthesis and minimal bibliographic links only. Synthetic cases use invented facts and `files: []`; they do not require donor complaints. Historic court outcomes remain outside the first-pass prompts. Validation checks reachability, continued existing functionality, privacy and installation; it does not claim LLM effectiveness.
