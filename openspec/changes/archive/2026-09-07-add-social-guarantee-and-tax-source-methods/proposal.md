## Why

New privately supplied pleadings and institutional opinions can improve reusable complaint reasoning when their procedural roles, exact issues and limits of the later judicial answer are preserved. Public attribution must credit confirmed sources without publishing original texts or missing-link notices.

- Review the six requested case groups, including PAG's parallel-import complaint and the distinction between an ordinary claim, constitutional complaints and opinions by different participants.

## Impact
