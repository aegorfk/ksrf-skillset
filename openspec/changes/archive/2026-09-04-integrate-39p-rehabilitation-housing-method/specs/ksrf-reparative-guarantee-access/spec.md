## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Special reparative entitlement is not ordinary assistance

The method MUST establish the special guarantee, eligible category, historical norm version, linked application and restorative purpose before comparing ordinary access conditions. Circular prerequisites MUST be tested against feasible alternatives and independent lawful refusal grounds. An administrative refusal, a claimant citation or a possible future condition MUST NOT alone prove judicial application of every linked norm.

#### Scenario: Return requires prior residence
- **WHEN** housing intended to enable return requires years of prior residence
- **THEN** the method tests the circular condition, eligibility and judicial causal chain rather than treating all residence rules as unconstitutional

#### Scenario: Record is incomplete
- **WHEN** an applied norm, eligible status or judicial reason is unproven
- **THEN** the corresponding conclusion remains unresolved and positive historical outcomes do not cure it

### Requirement: Historical comparison preserves remedy and actor boundaries

The retrospective card MUST separate complaint hypotheses, majority holding, separate opinion and later implementation. It MUST distinguish registration, housing allocation, legislative correction and individual review. Queue calculations MUST remain dated conditional scenarios rather than personal predictions. Current norm versions and procedural gates MUST be checked independently.

#### Scenario: Immediate housing is inferred
- **WHEN** a user infers immediate allocation or retrospective priority from paragraph 3 of the operative part
- **THEN** the method limits that paragraph to registration for the specified category and checks legislative and individual consequences separately

#### Scenario: Separate opinion is quoted as the Court
- **WHEN** a passage comes from the appended individual opinion
- **THEN** it is attributed to its author and is not presented as the majority holding

### Requirement: Public credit does not publish source documents

Public attribution MUST link the exact complaint, professional donor and final act, identify the applicant and confirmed representative, and avoid inferring sole authorship from a signature or PDF metadata. Source files and full-text derivatives MUST remain outside publication. Synthetic eval inputs MUST need no historical originals and MUST remain source-only.

#### Scenario: Public complaint exists
- **WHEN** the supplied complaint matches a publicly accessible document
- **THEN** the repository credits and links it without copying the PDF, OCR or reconstructive derivative

#### Scenario: New user lacks the original
- **WHEN** a clean-room user follows the new method
- **THEN** the method works with the new matter's primary materials and the installed card, without evals or historical originals
