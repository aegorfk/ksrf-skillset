## Why

The complaint presentation guidance defines structure and advocacy style but does not yet require the court-facing text to connect each material constitutional proposition with the precise judicial passage supporting it. Internal evidence traceability alone does not make that reasoning visible to the reader of the complaint.

## What Changes

- Add a substantive citation rule to the existing complaint presentation reference.
- Require an adjacent, accurate KSRF authority with date, number and precise locator for material constitutional propositions supported by case law.
- Permit short exact quotations, parenthetical references or footnotes under a consistent document-level convention.
- Preserve the distinction between the Court's reasoning, applicant analogy, Supreme Court guidance and internal review metadata.
- Add a focused QA cross-reference and describe the capability in the README.

## Capabilities

### Modified Capabilities
- `ksrf-complaint-presentation`: make source-bound legal reasoning visible in the complaint's substantive prose.

## Impact

Documentation and methodology only. No renderer, schema, test behavior, legal readiness gate or filing authority changes. No case-specific facts or unverified decisions are included.
