# Causal stage reasoning

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Upstream normative causality
The autonomous method SHALL distinguish a terminal barrier from the qualifying criterion that selects it and SHALL preserve alternative hypotheses when the criterion is unobservable.

#### Scenario: A category selects a shorter review period
- WHEN a provided decision applies a short period because of a contested category
- THEN the method checks both the category criterion and the period without inventing missing findings.

### Requirement: Whole regime comparison
The method SHALL compare relevant advantages and burdens within each regime rather than silently combining their favorable parts.

#### Scenario: Longer duration accompanies a smaller payment
- WHEN one regime pays less but lasts longer
- THEN the longer duration is assessed as a counterargument, not ignored or treated as an automatic justification.

### Requirement: Stage and pair boundaries
The method SHALL distinguish wrong pairing, unavailable stages and reasoning errors and SHALL keep later sources outside the frozen prediction input.

#### Scenario: A later appeal introduces a new ground
- WHEN the runner only received the first-instance decision
- THEN absence of the later ground is recorded as an observability limit, not silently repaired with the appeal.

#### Scenario: Pair identity conflicts
- WHEN the two sides concern different claims despite matching court and date
- THEN the separate evaluator requests identity review without reassigning the pair or treating the difference as a model error.
