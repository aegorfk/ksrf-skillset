# Design

Use `ksrf-argument-patterns/references/universal-argument-workflow.md` and its existing open catalog of 22 operations. Comparative work chooses a structural question before consulting topic-specific source cards. The source case is evidence of the operation in its own context, not a mandatory template for a new case.

Update the comparative SKILL, workbook, transfer bridge and method-catalog introduction, with short links from argument-patterns and rights-argument-builder. Replace the wage-only public invocation with a subject-neutral request. Keep the wage worked example explicitly conditional in the transfer bridge; preserve all original source facts, quotations and provenance records.

Candidate extraction is audited for topic filtering; do not change its executable bytes unless an actual topic constraint is found. Acquisition/vector default priorities are a separate code change and release. No corpus migration or deletion of topical collections is needed.

Verify source provenance and installed package identity. A separate agent receives fictional non-wage case materials and only the necessary revised instructions, without expected outcomes. Evaluate observable reasoning and gaps; a small synthetic check does not establish legal effectiveness across all disputes.
