## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: One intervention on identical real input
The experiment SHALL compare Hn-1 and Hn on identical frozen real dossiers and fixed model, prompt, budget and legal temporal context, with separate fresh runners and immutable cumulative overlays.

#### Scenario: A previously viewed dossier is selected
- **WHEN** an author has already inspected its complaint or outcome
- **THEN** it is labelled development/regression and cannot be called an independent held-out case

#### Scenario: Diagnostic approval excludes substantive metrics
- **WHEN** a record is diagnostic-only or its source scope is unresolved
- **THEN** it is excluded from the substantive denominator until the proper scope is established

### Requirement: Authentic execution and blinded human judgment
Each meaningful model experiment SHALL have actual outputs, authenticated Langfuse readback and DeepEval contract results; substantive comparison SHALL additionally require independent human legal review with concealed method version and frozen pre-reveal decisions.

#### Scenario: Automated checks pass without a human review
- **WHEN** only machine results or source audits exist
- **THEN** legal-quality effect remains unmeasured and no confirmed improvement is reported

### Requirement: Negative findings and bounded inference
The report SHALL retain adverse changes, ties, insufficient evidence, reviewer disagreement and scope limits, and distinguish incremental from cumulative effects.

#### Scenario: Better style introduces a legal defect
- **WHEN** a candidate is more readable but invents an authority, hides an adverse condition or requests unsupported powers
- **THEN** the material defect is reported independently and cannot be offset by style or aggregate score

#### Scenario: Only one real case is reviewed
- **WHEN** a case-level comparison is complete
- **THEN** the report does not claim general or significant legal-quality gains without the separately specified external validation
