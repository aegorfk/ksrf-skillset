# Refine complaint prose and prayer layout

## Why
The applicant's position must present affirmative legal arguments rather than research questions. The concluding section must distinguish the author's legal basis, the prayer marker and the numbered requests. Authorities currently appear as a smaller left-aligned inset despite forming part of the argument.

## What Changes
- Specify affirmative prose in section II while retaining research questions in internal analysis.
- Render an optional author-supplied `request_basis` in section III, followed by a separate centered prayer marker, the selected numbered requests and annexes.
- Give authority paragraphs the same body typography, preserving their source identity and bindings.
- Align the cycle, facts/demands, rights-builder and QA instructions and add synthetic regressions.

## Impact
This changes presentation and editorial guidance only. It does not supply legal grounds, rewrite claims, choose requests, change sentence roles or grant filing authority. Private complaint text and identifying case details are excluded.
