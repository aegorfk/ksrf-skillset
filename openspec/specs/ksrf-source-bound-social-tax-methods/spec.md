# ksrf-source-bound-social-tax-methods Specification

## Purpose
Define source-bound methods for social guarantees, legal-effect comparisons, differentiated sanctions and personal tax-harm liability, with separate participant roles and self-contained synthetic validation.
## Requirements
### Requirement: Preserve source role and the narrow judicial answer
The skillset SHALL separate each supplied source's procedural role, proposed argument, subsequent judicial answer and limits of transfer. Public summaries SHALL omit private originals and missing-source-link notices, while preserving confirmed credit and available source links.

#### Scenario: An ordinary claim precedes a court request
- **WHEN** a supplied claim belongs to a case later referred by a court
- **THEN** the method distinguishes the claim from the constitutional request and does not invent a personal constitutional complaint
- **AND** a later relevant family-status condition is not dismissed because the initial pleading considered it immaterial

#### Scenario: A procedural guarantee must precede the adverse act
- **WHEN** later judicial authorization is offered as a cure for an earlier dismissal
- **THEN** the method separately assesses timing, the guarantee's function, illegality and available relief
- **AND** it does not infer automatic reinstatement from a favorable constitutional interpretation

#### Scenario: Reorganization or a legal fiction creates an extra condition
- **WHEN** interpretation makes a benefit depend on administrative re-establishment or turns document equivalence into a new payment obligation
- **THEN** the method identifies the exact legal effect, functional purpose and material distinguishing facts
- **AND** it tests the adverse interpretation without treating all reorganization or tax disputes as unconstitutional

#### Scenario: Multiple opinions address personal tax-loss liability
- **WHEN** several institutions and experts submit materially different positions
- **THEN** the method keeps their roles, disagreement, causal premises, collection sequence and proposed guarantees separate
- **AND** it does not treat a non-rehabilitating criminal termination as a conviction or an institutional opinion as the Court's holding

#### Scenario: A valid prohibition has disproportionate consequences
- **WHEN** an original product and a counterfeit receive identical sanctions
- **THEN** the method tests separate consequences, comparable harm and safety exceptions without assuming that the underlying prohibition is invalid
- **AND** a historical parallel-import judgment does not establish the current import regime

### Requirement: Validate transfer without private originals
Evaluation scenarios SHALL use fictional facts that are sufficient to exercise the method without private complaints, OCR or historical outcome access. Passing structural tests SHALL NOT be described as demonstrated legal effectiveness.

#### Scenario: A user has only the installed skills and new case materials
- **WHEN** the user applies these methods
- **THEN** the runtime references and synthetic scenario expectations are self-contained within their respective runtime/source profiles
- **AND** references to historical materials supply provenance rather than a dependency on the private research corpus
