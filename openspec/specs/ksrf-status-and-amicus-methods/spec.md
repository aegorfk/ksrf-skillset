# ksrf-status-and-amicus-methods Specification

## Purpose
Provide source-bound methods for status incompatibility arguments and independent amicus analysis, preserving court-outcome limits, accurate public attribution and source-free evaluation.
## Requirements
### Requirement: Status incompatibility analysis preserves the normative mechanism
The skill MUST distinguish the status, actual functions, permanent or non-permanent basis, applied prohibition, suspension, termination and the specific consequence. A qualified silence finding MUST require affirmative support from the normative system and the exact court holding, not mere absence of a prohibition.

#### Scenario: A lesser involvement receives a harsher consequence
- **WHEN** a synthetic record provides temporary suspension for permanent service but threatened termination for non-permanent service
- **THEN** the skill tests the justification of this distinction and alternative explanations without declaring every incompatibility restriction unconstitutional

#### Scenario: A favourable interpretation is mistaken for invalidation
- **WHEN** a later court act preserves the norm subject to an interpretation and orders review
- **THEN** the skill distinguishes that outcome from invalidation, completed review and permission to combine every public position

### Requirement: Amicus interpretation remains distinct from court authority
The skill MUST classify each amicus by its actual matter, date, authors, submission role and interpretation method. Historical, systemic, evolutionary and comparative arguments MUST retain their evidence limits and may not override express text or substitute for current procedural authority.

#### Scenario: Unrelated materials share an inbox
- **WHEN** an amicus and a complaint concern different applicants, years and proceedings
- **THEN** they remain separate sources with independent outcome links and credits

#### Scenario: A proposed remedy was not adopted
- **WHEN** the amicus proposes differentiated measures and the later court limits permissible implementation
- **THEN** the proposal is retained as an author argument rather than presented as the court's adopted rule or completed enforcement

### Requirement: Public credits and source-free evaluation preserve privacy
The release MUST include active source, professional donor and final-act links where verified, distinguishing a professional homepage from an unavailable exact complaint publication. Public methods and synthetic evals MUST contain no originals, OCR, personal source paths or reconstructive text. Attribution MUST separate authors, acknowledged contributors, material providers and signatories.

#### Scenario: A user installs without historical complaints
- **WHEN** the new cards are installed and the synthetic cases are reviewed
- **THEN** the cards remain usable without historical source files, eval assets stay source-only and no test result is described as proven judicial effectiveness
