# ksrf-nonparticipant-enforcement-method Specification

## Purpose
Define the source-safe method for identifying enforcement barriers affecting nonparticipants in an earlier KSRF proceeding, separating interim protection, noncontinuation of execution and reopening while preserving current-law and human-review gates.
## Requirements
### Requirement: Nonparticipant enforcement method separates effects and dates

The installed method MUST distinguish the general binding norm meaning, individual reopening, noncontinuation of enforcement and interim protection. It MUST record the person's status in each constitutional proceeding, the norm and applied meaning, the state of execution at the relevant earlier KSRF decision and now, both procedural refusal chains, current legal basis, competent body and requested effect. Missing application or execution evidence MUST remain unresolved. It MUST NOT equate cessation of enforcement with cancellation of the underlying judgment or restoration of title.

#### Scenario: Public-owner eviction remains partly unexecuted
- **WHEN** a nonparticipant invokes a prior KSRF ruling against continuing eviction
- **THEN** the method tests every condition of the ruling, separates protection until reopening from final disposal of the judgment, and requires independent current procedural verification

#### Scenario: Private creditor or completed execution is substituted
- **WHEN** the same argument is applied to a private creditor or a judgment fully executed before the relevant KSRF ruling
- **THEN** the special 30-P/2020 route is not treated as established and another independent basis is required

#### Scenario: Notification is mistaken for suspension
- **WHEN** a complaint is filed or accepted and notification under article 98 is requested
- **THEN** notification and a separate competent decision on suspension remain distinct, and neither filing nor notification is described as automatic suspension

### Requirement: Complaint method and attribution remain public-safe

The package MUST preserve external links to the exact published complaint, the donor's professional page and the official outcome. It MUST credit Grigory Viktorovich Vaypan as the documented representative and signatory and ILPP as the professional source. It MUST NOT publish the complaint, embedded motion, extracted full text, page images or reconstructive derivatives. Runtime cards MUST remain usable without the historical source and require each new matter's own primary documents.

#### Scenario: Cleanroom user has no historical complaints
- **WHEN** the skills are installed without development data
- **THEN** the method and question checklist are present, while originals and development evals are absent

#### Scenario: Synthetic evaluation is prepared
- **WHEN** scenarios are added for partial execution, completed execution, private creditor, notification or missing evidence
- **THEN** they use independent synthetic facts, do not expose the source and are not described as model runs or predictive validation

### Requirement: Scope a legal counterexample to the actual enforcement objection

The enforcement methodology SHALL distinguish the refutation of an alleged necessary condition from the independent justification of the requested protection, using source-bound adversarial materials without importing another procedure's conditions.

#### Scenario: The objection asserts that termination requires cancellation
- **WHEN** a supplied objection treats cancellation of the judgment as necessary for termination of enforcement
- **THEN** the method checks the scope and context of that assertion and any verified counterexample
- **AND** a counterexample removes only the asserted universal dependency, not the need to justify protection in the applicant's case

#### Scenario: The objection denies an automatic effect
- **WHEN** a source denies automatic termination or a specific statutory ground
- **THEN** the method does not rewrite that narrower statement as universal impossibility or an admission of the applicant's entire position

#### Scenario: A settlement is used as a counterexample
- **WHEN** the historical applicants' response invokes a settlement during enforcement
- **THEN** the method preserves the distinct legal basis and consent conditions and does not import them into a dispute lacking those conditions
- **AND** current authority, constitutional necessity, competent procedure and remaining creditor interests still require separate verification

#### Scenario: Source and test limits remain visible
- **WHEN** the clarification is distributed
- **THEN** it links the real objection and response with their separate roles and page locators without distributing source documents
- **AND** editorial tests are not represented as a legal-quality benchmark or permission to file
