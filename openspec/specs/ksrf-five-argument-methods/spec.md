# ksrf-five-argument-methods Specification

## Purpose

Provide five ordered, source-bound candidate transformations for constitutional complaint arguments while preserving independent legal review, effective lawful relief and honest uncertainty. Comparative quality evaluation is governed separately and remains unmeasured until completed.

## Requirements

### Requirement: Ordered candidate argument transformations
The skills SHALL expose H1 through H5 in the approved implementation order and SHALL label them as candidate research and revision methods, not demonstrated legal-quality gains or mandatory constitutional tests.

#### Scenario: Existing methods already cover generic questions
- **WHEN** the new reference is used alongside existing interpretation, objection, proportionality and remedy workbooks
- **THEN** it adds a discriminating transformation with a concrete output rather than replacing their authority or human-review gates

### Requirement: H1 decisive premise across strong readings
The method SHALL reconstruct both readings with source roles, identify their minimum material premise difference and test the consequence of both confirming and refuting the proposed discriminant.

#### Scenario: Discriminant does not alter the disputed result
- **WHEN** the same adverse result remains independently supported
- **THEN** the method records that the proposed discriminant is not decisive for that result and does not manufacture victory

### Requirement: H2 adverse position and surviving request
The method SHALL identify the strongest material adverse court position, its necessary conditions, exceptions and currentness; distinguish it from a party submission; and derive what remains of the applicant's request if the adverse position is accepted.

#### Scenario: Original adverse act is unavailable
- **WHEN** a party paraphrases a judicial position but the original cannot be verified
- **THEN** that paraphrase stays in the party lane and the dependent holding and distinction remain unverified without blocking independent drafting

#### Scenario: Later development exceeds the historical cutoff
- **WHEN** a relevant subsequent act postdates the frozen historical analysis cutoff
- **THEN** it is reserved for a separate prospective refresh and does not contaminate the outcome-blind input

### Requirement: H3 disputed element justification
The method SHALL preserve the supported purpose while examining the incremental contribution, burden, safeguards and supported alternatives for the disputed element; it SHALL permit findings to weaken the applicant's argument.

#### Scenario: Removing an element sacrifices the supported goal
- **WHEN** an alleged alternative lacks comparable effectiveness or legal feasibility
- **THEN** the alternative remains unproven and is not treated as proof of unconstitutionality

### Requirement: H4 real boundary rule testing
The method SHALL freeze a general candidate rule and relevance conditions before selecting real boundary records, record negative search coverage and rerun all prior boundary records after a material rule revision.

#### Scenario: No adequate exclusion comparator is available
- **WHEN** only supportive or materially incomparable records are found
- **THEN** the exclusion boundary stays untested, no fictional case is substituted, and a research draft may continue without a universality claim

### Requirement: H5 guarantee and mechanism separation
The method SHALL derive a source-bound necessary guarantee independently of the preferred implementation, test substitute mechanisms and preserve effective relief within verified competence.

#### Scenario: Narrow relief preserves the defect
- **WHEN** the narrower route does not deliver the supported guarantee in time
- **THEN** the method rejects automatic narrowing and investigates another lawful effective route without inventing powers

### Requirement: Non-reconstructive delivery and review
The release SHALL include only reusable methodology and technical checks, retain existing filing gates and keep source-bound dossiers and review outputs outside the public repository.

#### Scenario: Technical checks pass
- **WHEN** validators and structural regression tests pass
- **THEN** publication may describe method delivery, but comparative legal-quality effect stays unmeasured until separately completed human evaluation
