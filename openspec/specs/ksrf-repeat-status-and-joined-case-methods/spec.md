## Requirements
### Requirement: Independent status and temporal analysis
The methodology MUST distinguish the legal classification of a restriction from its qualifying events, duration and temporal operation. A non-punitive classification MUST NOT establish that every prior event qualifies or that temporal objections have been resolved.

#### Scenario: Several proceedings have different outcomes
- **WHEN** a restriction relies on multiple prior proceedings and some ended without a qualifying sanction or outside the legally relevant period
- **THEN** the workflow verifies each event, outcome and time boundary before counting repeat liability

### Requirement: Joined-case outcome attribution
The workflow MUST bind each argument and outcome to its applicant, challenged provision, reasoning and operative clause. It MUST NOT transfer a ruling on a limitation period to a different eligibility restriction merely because both occur in one judgment.

#### Scenario: One judgment decides several requests
- **WHEN** a personal complaint is joined with a broader institutional request
- **THEN** the comparison separates submitted claims, adopted reasoning, rejected reasoning and conditional individual consequences

### Requirement: Conditional individual review
The execution route MUST verify the divergence of the applied interpretation from the Court's interpretation and any other procedural obstacles required by the operative clause. Historical success MUST NOT be represented as completed individual recovery.

#### Scenario: A norm survives with a limiting interpretation
- **WHEN** review is ordered only if the original decision used a divergent interpretation and no other obstacles exist
- **THEN** the workflow records each condition separately and does not promise cancellation, repayment or a current filing route without supporting evidence

### Requirement: Private source and portable scenarios
The release MUST keep the complaint and reconstructive derivatives private and provide self-contained synthetic scenarios without names, case identifiers or source-file dependencies.

#### Scenario: The user has no historical complaint
- **WHEN** the supplied scenario is run on a clean installation
- **THEN** every fact needed for the exercise is contained in its fictional input, and structural checks are not represented as measured model performance
