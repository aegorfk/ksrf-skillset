## Requirements
### Requirement: Changed norms receive a source-bound substantive comparison

The skill MUST compare the identified former and successor regulation when a rule
relevant to the complaint has changed, been repealed, replaced or renumbered,
or has acquired a different established judicial meaning,
across addressees, conditions, exceptions, legal effects, transition provisions and
judicial meaning, using exact editions and official sources. It MUST record material
unknowns and limit conclusions to the elements actually established.

#### Scenario: The article number changes but the relevant mechanism survives

- **WHEN** official texts and application evidence establish only a renumbering of
  the challenged mechanism
- **THEN** the analysis explains the unchanged identified elements without treating
  the new number as a new constitutional question or as proof of admissibility

#### Scenario: A new safeguard corrects the successor rule

- **WHEN** the successor adds a legally effective safeguard addressing the identified
  mechanism
- **THEN** the skill distinguishes that change from the legal treatment of earlier
  relations and the individual consequences still borne by the applicant

### Requirement: Continued application and remaining harm remain separate

The methodology MUST separately establish past judicial application, a legal basis
and case evidence for continued application to earlier relations, and remaining
individual harm. Neither harm alone nor textual continuity MUST imply continued
application, constitutionality, admissibility, exhaustion or a renewed filing period.
The exact applied edition MUST NOT be silently replaced by a successor provision.

A general temporal rule combined with established dates MAY support continued
application. The method MUST NOT universally demand a special transitional clause,
a new judicial act or a factually pending procedure as an additional prerequisite.

#### Scenario: Harm remains but continued application is not established

- **WHEN** the applicant retains an adverse consequence but the governing transition
  rule or continued application is unknown
- **THEN** the analysis identifies the missing evidence and does not treat the
  remaining consequence as a completed admissibility finding

#### Scenario: Old law continues to govern the applicant's relations

- **WHEN** the transition rule and judicial materials establish continued application
  of the former provision
- **THEN** the analysis supplies that evidence to a separate current admissibility
  review, without declaring the complaint admissible or well founded

### Requirement: The method supports bounded drafting and challenge

The installed reference MUST include official-source case analyses with precise
locators, a limiting or adverse example, clearly synthetic drafting examples, and
the strongest counterargument. Historical doctrine MUST remain distinct from current
law. Conditional integration MUST preserve unrelated methodology and existing runtime
contracts; source-only evaluation materials MUST stay out of the user installation.

#### Scenario: A historical doctrinal account is invoked

- **WHEN** a 2017 account describes review despite changed legislation
- **THEN** the skill treats it as a research lead and checks current statutory and
  official-case support before drawing any procedural conclusion

#### Scenario: A new matter is evaluated independently

- **WHEN** an independent evaluator receives the candidate method and previously
  withheld raw synthetic inputs
- **THEN** the result is assessed for temporal, causal and procedural reasoning rather
  than merely reproducing the method's labels or matching text
