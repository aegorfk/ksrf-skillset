# ksrf-cross-instance-causal-drafting Specification

## Purpose

Guide source-bound causal complaint drafting across judicial stages, separating
norm use, actual alternative grounds and the consequence alleged by the applicant.
## Requirements
### Requirement: Judicial use and causal consequence remain distinct

The methodology MUST distinguish actual court-authored norm use, the norm's causal
role, preservation of the argument and independent grounds. It MUST NOT equate a
citation with operative use or erase recorded use merely because another ground
may preserve the outcome.

#### Scenario: An expressly used norm is not necessary for the same outcome

- **WHEN** a court used the challenged rule but an established independent ground
  may be sufficient for the same outcome
- **THEN** the analysis preserves the use evidence, separately tests causal harm,
  and does not claim that the court never used the rule

### Requirement: The chain of judicial reasons is source bound

The methodology MUST follow adoption, replacement, concurrency and uncertainty
across the available full judicial acts. It MUST distinguish independent grounds
from grounds dependent on the challenged mechanism and MUST NOT infer incorporation
merely from an unchanged disposition.

#### Scenario: A later court changes the reason but keeps the result

- **WHEN** a later act replaces or limits an earlier ground
- **THEN** the complaint paragraph identifies that change and limits the causal
  claim to the grounds and consequences that remain supported

#### Scenario: The alternative ground depends on the challenged evidentiary rule

- **WHEN** the supposedly independent ground depends on a burden, presumption or
  exclusion produced by the challenged norm
- **THEN** the analysis makes that dependency explicit instead of treating two
  differently worded reasons as necessarily independent

#### Scenario: A court describes a general independent ground

- **WHEN** a source describes a legal ground without establishing its trigger
  for this applicant, while a recited party statement disputes the trigger
- **THEN** the analysis requires case-specific evidence of the trigger and court
  reliance, preserves each speaker's role and does not infer either an actual
  independent ground or an adjudicated absence from those statements alone

### Requirement: The method improves bounded complaint drafting

The installed guidance MUST provide usable causal-paragraph examples, a strongest
objection, official-source examples with limits, and a concrete next step for a
material gap. Small synthetic evaluations MUST NOT be presented as general legal
accuracy or filing authority. No new machine gate or schema is required.

#### Scenario: The record cannot resolve the causal chain

- **WHEN** a missing full act or passage could change the chain analysis
- **THEN** the output identifies the missing evidence and uses a bounded or
  unresolved conclusion without inventing the applicant's facts or court reasons

### Requirement: Remand reasoning is not inferred from disposition alone

The methodology MUST distinguish the remanding court's express interpretation,
identified defect, unresolved questions, and the subsequent court's own reasons.
It MUST NOT attribute a substantive criterion to a court solely because that court
remanded rather than entered another disposition or later refused transfer.

#### Scenario: A party infers a criterion from the remedy not chosen

- **WHEN** the complaint argues that a court must have adopted a criterion because
  otherwise it would have left an earlier judgment in force
- **THEN** the review treats this as a party inference, seeks the actual reasoning
  and applicable procedural powers, and preserves any unresolved interpretation

#### Scenario: A court requires reasons for rejecting an earlier finding

- **WHEN** a remand identifies missing reasons for rejecting a finding
- **THEN** the review does not equate that instruction alone with a new substantive
  eligibility condition or a predetermined finding on the merits

#### Scenario: Only part of the judicial chain is available

- **WHEN** a full remand act is available but the subsequent full act is missing
- **THEN** the output can state the remanding court's supported conclusions while
  leaving subsequent adoption, departure, causal effect and chain completeness open
