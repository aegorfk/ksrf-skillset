# ksrf-confidentiality-and-expert-opinions Specification

## Purpose
Provide source-bound methods for confidentiality safeguards, individual access to justice and official publication, while preserving expert attribution, historical limits and private-source-safe evaluation.
## Requirements
### Requirement: Functional safeguards and separate challenged provisions
The skillset SHALL test the protected information, purpose, authorised objects, safeguards and lawful exceptions before transferring a precedent between investigative procedures, and SHALL classify each challenged provision independently.

#### Scenario: Safeguard survives procedure relabelling
- **WHEN** a provided case changes the procedural label but retains access to the same protected information and a verified common protective regime
- **THEN** the method requires an explicit normative bridge and examines the safeguards, without treating a court order alone as sufficient or claiming absolute immunity

#### Scenario: Procedure does not regulate the challenged substantive ground
- **WHEN** a second challenged article regulates only the hearing procedure rather than the alleged power to alter an earlier decision
- **THEN** that dependency remains unproven without invalidating the independent confidentiality argument

### Requirement: Source-bound expert analysis
The skillset SHALL distinguish authors, donor, addressee and court, and SHALL separately test the individual's protected interest, forum, remedy, official publication and temporal applicability.

#### Scenario: Expert versions and missing attribution
- **WHEN** one opinion names two authors with separate versions and another has no author block
- **THEN** the named versions retain their attribution and the donor is not automatically declared sole author of the unsigned opinion

#### Scenario: Access is narrower than unrestricted challenge
- **WHEN** an individual's interest is distinct from a collective organisation's and a specific procedural exclusion is established
- **THEN** a narrow access argument may proceed independently, without universal standing or automatic admissibility before the Constitutional Court

#### Scenario: Text is online
- **WHEN** a normative text is available online
- **THEN** the method verifies the legally designated publication channel, complete text, relevant date and applicable version instead of equating availability with official publication or rejecting every electronic publication

### Requirement: Private-source-safe validation and release
The release SHALL contain only abstracted methods, public attribution and self-contained synthetic scenarios, and SHALL preserve conditional historical outcomes separately from proof of actual restoration.

#### Scenario: User has no source complaint
- **WHEN** the source-only evaluations run without the donated files
- **THEN** all new scenarios have complete synthetic inputs and no original-file dependencies

#### Scenario: Conditional reopening
- **WHEN** a determination relies on an intervening precedent and directs conditional reconsideration
- **THEN** the method checks chronology and the exact remedy instead of reporting automatic success, general entitlement for later applicants or completed restoration
