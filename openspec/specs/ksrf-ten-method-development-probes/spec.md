# ksrf-ten-method-development-probes Specification

## Purpose

Define a private, preregistered development pilot for ten source-bound argument operations, with concealed comparisons, honest unknowns and authenticated result accounting distinct from legal approval.

## Requirements

### Requirement: Preregistered source-bound paired development probes
The maintainer workflow SHALL freeze ten hypotheses, real-source packets, model settings, rubric and run limits before producing matched baseline and single-intervention outputs. It SHALL label this known-source development pilot separately from outcome-blind and human legal evaluation.

#### Scenario: Existing coverage already performs the intervention
- **WHEN** baseline and candidate both preserve the required legal distinction
- **THEN** a tie or null effect is retained and no runtime expansion is presumed necessary

#### Scenario: Source coverage does not support a boundary test
- **WHEN** available real records are materially incomparable or a required primary act is unavailable
- **THEN** the dependent test remains untested or unknown without fictitious facts, substituted source roles or a universal-rule claim

### Requirement: Concealed review and honest metric scope
The workflow SHALL compare concealed outputs in both orders using fresh source-bound model reviews, preserve critical defects and unknowns, and execute explicit DeepEval diagnostics without silently selecting a different provider. Mechanical validation SHALL NOT be represented as semantic grounding or independent human assessment.

#### Scenario: Order-dependent preference
- **WHEN** reversed presentation changes the preferred arm
- **THEN** the hypothesis is reported as order-sensitive rather than a robust candidate gain

### Requirement: Private receipts and authenticated observability
The workflow SHALL keep reconstructive source and answer artifacts outside Git, bind prompts and outputs to hashes, retain usage and nullable costs, authenticate an experiment-only Langfuse trace before trials, and read back per-run trace and score metadata before reporting instrumentation complete.

#### Scenario: Trace unavailable or legal trial fails
- **WHEN** a trace cannot be read back or a trial produces a legal defect
- **THEN** the receipt preserves the failure, no production prompt is changed, and no replacement answer is silently selected
